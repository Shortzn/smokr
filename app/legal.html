Purpose and basic editorial direction

Smokr helps travelers find legal smoking areas in Japan. It shows information from official city and ward lists, OpenStreetMap and, once enabled, reviewed contributions from visitors. The information may be incomplete or outdated. Please follow signs and local rules on site.

Contributions from visitors

Places, reports and photos sent by visitors are reviewed before they are published. To report problematic content, use “Report photo” in the app or write to the email address above. Published content can be withdrawn at any time.

Privacy

Short version: Smokr needs no account and uses no tracking, analytics or ads. Your settings and your own feedback (“Found it”, “Not found”) stay on your device.

Using the map

  • The app is delivered by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any website, GitHub processes technical data of each request, such as the IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which receives the same kind of technical request data.
  • Your location is only requested when you tap the location button. It is used on your device to sort the list and is not sent to Smokr.
  • Settings, your feedback and the last map position are stored in your browser's local storage. You can delete them by clearing the website data in your browser.

When you send a place, a report or a photo

This part applies once the community features are enabled in the app.

  • What is stored: what you enter (position, type, access, name, notes, comment), the language of the app and, if you add one, your photo. Location and other metadata are removed from photos, in the browser and again on the server.
  • Private test phase: while the community is in a private test, sending requires a personal test code. The app keeps it in your browser's local storage. The server stores which code was used for a contribution, not who you are.
  • Abuse protection: to prevent spam, a hash of your IP address is stored. It is salted and changes daily, so it cannot be linked across days, and it is deleted after 30 days. Submissions are checked with Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes technical data of your browser. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Where: in a database of Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers and D1). Request logs are not stored permanently.
  • Publication: approved contributions are public and licensed under CC BY 4.0. Rejected contributions and photos are deleted. Photos that were never reviewed are deleted after 90 days.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and the legitimate interest in a service free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
